Company overview

Rooted in Tema.
Ready to serve across sectors.

ELISHGANDY LIMITED COMPANY is a registered multidisciplinary services organization focused on quality service delivery, regulatory compliance and sustainable development.

Headquartered in Tema, Ghana, the organization serves public- and private-sector clients across multiple sectors. Its operations are guided by professionalism, accountability, quality, safety, regulatory compliance and continuous improvement.

Our eight divisions bring a broad service scope together within one organization. The precise approach for each engagement is aligned with the client's requirements and the applicable context.

Business philosophy

Understand the need. Organize the work. Deliver responsibly.

We believe dependable service starts with understanding client requirements and coordinating the right people and resources around them.

Our approach emphasizes clear communication, professional oversight, risk awareness and quality control. Where a requirement calls for qualified personnel or strategic partners, these are considered as part of the delivery approach.
