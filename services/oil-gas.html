Division overview

Support activities around demanding operations.

The Oil and Gas Support Services division covers support activities for petroleum and natural gas extraction, including field logistics and auxiliary operational support.

We communicate this scope carefully: the company provides support services and does not claim to directly extract petroleum or natural gas. Any engagement is subject to the client's operational requirements, relevant approvals and applicable standards.

Service areas

Auxiliary support, planned to the scope.

Field logistics

Logistics support considered around field schedules, access needs and operational coordination.

Auxiliary operations

Support activities that contribute to the coordination of petroleum and natural gas operations.

Service coordination

Structured communication and resource coordination aligned with client-defined requirements.

Requirement alignment

Attention to the operational context, applicable standards, safety expectations and procurement conditions.

Industry context

Coordination matters in the field.

Industrial environments require attention to the client's systems, safety expectations, schedules and regulatory context.

Scope, operating requirements and any mandatory qualifications should be confirmed before a service is proposed. No operating site, extraction activity, client relationship or industry certification is represented as company evidence.
