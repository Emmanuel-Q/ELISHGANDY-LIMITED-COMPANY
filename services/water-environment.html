Division overview

Infrastructure that supports communities and operations.

The Water, Environment and Utilities division covers water supply systems, sewerage systems, water management and remediation activities.

Our approach recognizes the importance of responsible water management, environmental protection and utility infrastructure. Specific services are defined by the project scope and applicable technical and regulatory requirements.

Service areas

Water and environmental support.

Water supply systems

Activities connected to water supply infrastructure and client-defined system requirements.

Sewerage systems

Support relating to sewerage systems, planned in accordance with the project scope.

Water management

Water management activities that consider operational requirements and responsible use.

Remediation activities

Remediation-related work considered against the relevant conditions, standards and requirements.

Industry context

Responsible water management matters.

Utility systems and remediation work require attention to the environment, public needs and the standards that govern each project.

Engineering credentials, treatment performance, project outcomes and regulatory approvals are not claimed on this page. These must be confirmed for each defined engagement.
