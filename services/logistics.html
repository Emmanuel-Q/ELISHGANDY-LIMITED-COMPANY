Division overview

Movement and coordination for business needs.

The Transportation and Logistics division includes transportation services, storage and logistics support.

We emphasize coordination, movement of goods, logistics support and operational reliability. The specific service scope is determined by the client's requirements and the resources available for the engagement.

Service areas

Support across movement and storage.

Transportation services

Transportation activities based on agreed delivery requirements and operating conditions.

Storage support

Storage-related services considered in line with the scope, handling requirements and operational needs.

Logistics support

Operational reliability

Attention to clear communication, scheduling and service coordination.
