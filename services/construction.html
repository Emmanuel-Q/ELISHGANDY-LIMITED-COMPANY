Division overview

Infrastructure support with a structured approach.

The Construction and Infrastructure Development division includes building construction, road and railway construction, utility construction and specialized construction activities.

We approach construction requirements with attention to project management, relevant expertise, resource allocation, risk management and quality control. The scale and nature of any proposed engagement must be confirmed against the client's scope.
