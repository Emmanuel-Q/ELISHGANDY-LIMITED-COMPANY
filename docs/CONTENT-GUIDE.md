# Content guide

## Approved profile

ELISHGANDY LIMITED COMPANY is a registered multidisciplinary services organization headquartered in Tema, Ghana. Its mission, vision, values, eight divisions, operational approach, QHSE commitments, and compliance positioning follow `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`.

## Accuracy rules

Do not invent company contacts, directors, registration identifiers, licenses, certifications, clients, testimonials, project history, personnel or equipment counts, financial capacity, partnerships, or other performance evidence. Images are illustrative industry photography only.

## Missing approvals

- Public production domain and website URL.
- Telephone and business email.
- Approved capability statement document.
- Actual company photographs and project evidence.
- Any verified registration, license, certification, or named leadership information intended for publication.
- Hosting deployment hostname, port, account username, and confirmed production/preview paths.

Keep unknown values as clearly marked placeholders until authorized facts are supplied.

## Photography

The illustrative Unsplash photographs are stored locally as optimized, descriptively named WebP files in `assets/images/` so deployed pages do not depend on remote image URLs. Image alternatives on meaningful content images describe what is shown; the filenames themselves do not replace alt text for screen-reader users. They are illustrative industry imagery only, not company projects or facilities. The site discloses this in the footer rather than overlaying every image. Use remains subject to the Unsplash License; replace images with approved company photography when available.
