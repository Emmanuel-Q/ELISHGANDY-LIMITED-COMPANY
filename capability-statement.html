Company overview

Multidisciplinary services from Tema, Ghana.

ELISHGANDY LIMITED COMPANY is a registered multidisciplinary services organization focused on quality service delivery, regulatory compliance and sustainable development.

Its service divisions span agriculture and agribusiness; oil and gas support; construction and infrastructure; real estate; water, environment and utilities; information technology and consultancy; publication and communication; and transportation and logistics.
