Who we are

One organization.
Eight service divisions.

ELISHGANDY LIMITED COMPANY is a registered multidisciplinary services organization focused on quality service delivery, regulatory compliance and sustainable development.

Headquartered in Tema, Ghana, we work across multiple sectors to provide reliable, professional and value-driven services to public- and private-sector clients. Our operations are guided by accountability, safety, quality and continuous improvement.
